Settings and hookup help

Hooking up the IC-746

  1. Easiest: set the iPad a foot from the speaker. Keep the room quiet.
  2. Cleanest: a cable from the radio's audio out into a USB-C headset adapter on the iPad, then pick it under Audio input.
  3. On the radio: CW mode, 500 Hz filter or wider, noise blanker off unless static is bad.

Leaving it running

Keep this page in front; the iPad stops the microphone if Safari goes to the background or the screen locks.

The page asks the iPad to keep the screen on while listening.

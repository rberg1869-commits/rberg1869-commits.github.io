Decoded CW shows up here in large type

  1. Run the 3.5 mm cable from the IC-746 rear EXT SP jack to the Saramonic adapter, and plug the adapter into the iPad.
  2. Turn the AF knob low, tune in a CW signal, then tap Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Raise AF until the Input meter reads about -30 to -15 dB. Tone tracks the signal by itself, or tap a signal on the waterfall.

No radio handy? Choose Test below for a practice signal.

Settings and hookup help

Wired to the IC-746

  1. IC-746 rear EXT SP jack, 3.5 mm cable, Saramonic SR-C2003, iPad USB-C port.
  2. The radio speaker goes quiet while EXT SP is in use; that is normal.
  3. Radio in CW mode, 500 Hz filter or wider, noise blanker off unless static is bad.

Leaving it running

Keep this page in front. iPadOS stops the audio if Safari goes to the background or the screen locks.

The page asks the iPad to keep the screen on while listening.

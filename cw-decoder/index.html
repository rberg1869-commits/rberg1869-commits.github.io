  1. Connect the radio audio to the iPad: 3.5 mm cable from the IC-746 rear EXT SP jack into the Saramonic, Saramonic into the iPad. Turn the AF knob low first.
  2. Tune in a CW signal, then tap Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Set the level so the Input meter reads about -30 to -15 dB. Tone tracks the signal by itself, or tap a signal on the waterfall.

Tap Auto QSO for automatic contacts (radio in USB; see Settings for the VOX hookup). No radio handy? Choose Practice for a make-believe band that answers back.

Each contact made on the air goes to your QRZ logbook; your logbook sync copies it to the others. Practice contacts are never sent.

Hooked to the IC-746

  1. Receive: rear EXT SP jack, 3.5 mm cable, Saramonic SR-C2003, iPad. AF knob low; raise it until Input reads -30 to -15 dB.
  2. Transmit today (no interface): radio in USB, VOX on, COMP off, MIC GAIN low, VOX delay 0.5 s (the radio must stay keyed through the space between words; the app keeps those spaces shorter than this). Set the hand mic right against the iPad speaker grille. Keep the shack quiet while Auto runs; room sound goes out while the radio is keyed.
  3. Level test: tick Transmit and SWR, then Send a level test. Use the iPad volume and MIC GAIN so ALC barely moves. If a cough or voice keys the radio, lower VOX GAIN.
  4. Later, with a SignaLink on ACC(1): choose SignaLink above; the mic and VOX are no longer needed.
  5. Check SWR on the radio after every band change; Auto will not transmit until you tick the box.

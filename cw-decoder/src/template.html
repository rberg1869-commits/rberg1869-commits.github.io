Decoded CW shows up here in large type

  1. Connect the radio audio to the iPad (Saramonic cable from the rear EXT SP jack, or the SignaLink on the rear ACC(1) socket).
  2. Tune in a CW signal, then tap Start listening and allow the microphone.
  3. Set the level so the Input meter reads about -30 to -15 dB. Tone tracks the signal by itself, or tap a signal on the waterfall.

Tap Auto QSO for automatic contacts. No radio handy? Choose Practice for a make-believe band that answers back.

Station (sent on the air)

Auto QSO

Each contact made on the air goes to your QRZ logbook; your logbook sync copies it to the others. Practice contacts are never sent.

Wired to the IC-746

  1. Receive only: rear EXT SP jack, 3.5 mm cable, Saramonic SR-C2003, iPad.
  2. Receive and transmit: SignaLink USB (SLUSB8PD) on the rear ACC(1) socket, USB to the iPad. Radio in USB mode for Auto QSO.
  3. Set the SignaLink TX knob so ALC barely moves on the level test. Keep the iPad volume where you set it.
  4. Check SWR on the radio after every band change; Auto will not transmit until you tick the box.
